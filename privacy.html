Last Card privacy policy

Effective 3 September 2026

What the game stores

Last Card stores your current game, finished realms, tutorial progress, and display settings in local storage on your device or in your browser. This lets a run resume where you left it.

What we collect

Nothing. Last Card has no account, analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry. The game does not transmit your save or personal information to us or to a third party.

Your control

Removing the app, or clearing this site's browser storage, removes the locally stored game data. Because we never receive that data, we cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

Changes

If a future version changes these practices, this page will be updated before that version is released.
